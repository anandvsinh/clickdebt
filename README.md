ClickDebt is a smart productivity tool that helps people understand how they spend their time on the internet which can also be explained by productivity‑oriented web platform integrated with a Chrome extension designed to analyze and improve users’ digital habits.. The extension tracks websites accessed by the user and accurately records the total time spent on each site, including repeated visits, in real time.
All this data is sent to a secure backend and shown on a personal dashboard after login.The dashboard provides clear insights through usage summaries, visual charts, and analytics. A unique feature of ClickDebt is its ability to convert time spent on websites into a monetary value, helping users better understand the real cost of their online behavior.

The goal of ClickDebt is by combining real‑time tracking, data visualization, and focus‑oriented features, ClickDebt aims to promote self‑awareness, reduce digital distractions, and encourage more effective time management.
